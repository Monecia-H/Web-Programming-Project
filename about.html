About Old Harbour High School

Old Harbour High School is a secondary school located at 33 South Street, Old Harbour, St. Catherine, Jamaica.

The school's motto is "In Pursuit of Excellence."

Our History

Old Harbour High School was established on October 12, 1969, as a Junior Secondary School. The school initially provided education for students in Grades 7 to 9.

It became a secondary school in 1977 and was upgraded to high school status in 1988. The school's first principal was Mrs. Enid Golding.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission: To provide quality education through relevant programmes, a safe and caring learning environment, and responsible educational practices.

Vision: To develop individuals into model citizens through quality education and personal growth.
